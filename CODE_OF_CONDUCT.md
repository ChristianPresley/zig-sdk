# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Everyone who takes part in this project must be respectful. Examples of good conduct:

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respect different points of view.
- Accept constructive feedback.
- Focus on what is best for the project.

Examples of conduct that is not accepted:

- Insults, harassment, or personal attacks.
- Publication of private information of other persons.
- Other conduct that a reasonable person finds inappropriate.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable conduct to the maintainer through a private GitHub message. The maintainer reviews every report and can remove comments, commits, or contributors.
